Project Red Vein

The footage is an art demostration of an encounter sequence from the game Project Red Vein, which is a conceptual first-person survival horror game set within the Outlast universe. Whiteridge Federal Penitentiary, the stage of the game, sits at the northernmost edge of the American mountain range — a maximum security facility carved into abandoned mineshafts, perpetually buried under snow and cut off from civilization. Originally built to house the nation's most dangerous criminals, it was quietly converted by the Murkoff Corporation into the site of Project Red Vein: a classified experiment attempting to fuse human subjects, simulating fungal mycelial network and engineering a distributed collective intelligence for military use.
